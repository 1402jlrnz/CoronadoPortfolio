# Rotoscope Project Step-by-Step Guide

## Step 1: Select Source Material
Choose your video source material. For guidance on selecting good source material for rotoscoping, watch this helpful YouTube tutorial:
[How to Choose Good Source Material for Rotoscoping](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=example)

## Step 2: Cut Video into 5-Second Clips
Using your preferred video editing software (CapCut, Adobe Premiere Pro, etc.), cut your source video into 5-second clips. This makes the rotoscoping process more manageable.

Example of a 5-second clip ready for rotoscoping:
![5-second clip example](image_example.jpg)

## Step 3: Drag Video Clip to Create New Composition
In Adobe After Effects:
1. Import your video clip (e.g., "week 2.mp4") into the Project panel
2. Drag the video file from the Project panel down to the timeline area
3. This will automatically create a new composition with your video
4. The composition will match your video's duration (5 seconds in this example)

The video should now appear in your composition window and timeline, ready for rotoscoping work.

## Step 4: Trace Each Layer
In Adobe After Effects:
1. Select the Pen Tool from the toolbar
2. Create a new solid layer for each element you want to trace (e.g., body, clothing, background)
3. Carefully trace around each element of your subject frame by frame
4. Use the roto brush tool for faster tracing on complex shapes
5. Add masks to isolate different parts of your subject

Example of traced layers:
![Traced layers example](traced_layers_example.jpg)

## Step 5: Add Colors Per Layer Using Fill
In Adobe After Effects:
1. Select each traced layer individually in the timeline
2. Go to Effect > Generate > Fill to apply the Fill effect
3. In the Effect Controls panel, choose your desired color for each layer
4. Adjust the fill opacity if needed (typically keep at 100% for solid colors)
5. Repeat for each layer to create a colorful rotoscoped animation

Example of colored layers:
![Colored layers example](colored_layers_example.jpg)

## Step 6: Add to Render Queue
In Adobe After Effects:
1. Go to Composition > Add to Render Queue or press Ctrl+Alt+/ (Cmd+Option+/ on Mac)
2. In the Render Queue panel, configure your output settings:
   - **Render Settings**: Best Settings (or customize as needed)
   - **Output Module**: Lossless (or choose format like H.264, QuickTime, etc.)
   - **Output To**: Click to choose your save location and filename
3. Click "Render" to start exporting your rotoscoped animation
4. Wait for the render to complete (progress bar will show completion status)

Your final rotoscoped video will be saved to your specified location!
