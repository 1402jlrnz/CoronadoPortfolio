Project Details

This 3D Solar System project demonstrates advanced 3D modeling and animation techniques with accurate astronomical data. Each celestial body includes real information about age, revolution periods, orbital periods, and distances from the Sun. The Time & Motion calculator allows users to explore how their age would differ on various planets based on orbital mechanics.

Technologies Used:

3D Modeling Animation Rendering Lighting Astronomical Data
